    One round from take-off to vanish

    Every round follows the same pattern. A short betting window opens, the stake is placed, and the curve starts rising from 1.00×. At a point fixed in advance by the random number generator, the plane leaves the screen and the round ends. Anyone who cashes out first keeps stake times the multiplier shown at that moment; anyone who waits too long loses the stake.

    Because the departure point is set before the round starts, nothing a player clicks can change it mid-flight. That is why thorfortune casino guidance always puts timing and bankroll control ahead of guesswork. Each round is separate, and past results do not influence the next one.

    Why players can audit results

    The original game uses a provably fair system. Before each round the server commits to a secret seed and publishes its hash, then the result is generated from that seed together with values contributed by the first players in the round.

    The published hash locks the departure point before take-off. Neither the operator nor the player can alter it once betting opens, and the result can be checked afterwards.

    After the flight ends, the seed is revealed and a checker can confirm that the hash matches the round result. Cloned versions do not always provide this level of transparency, which is why many players prefer licensed sites and a proper thorfortune casino review before signing up.

    • Set a session budget before the first round and stop when it is gone, regardless of the history ribbon.
